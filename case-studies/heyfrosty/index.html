THE PROBLEM

Advice is not work.

Chat assistants end every conversation the same way: with a suggestion and your name on the follow-through. The recap still has to be written, the inbox still has to be triaged, the follow-ups still have to go out. The bottleneck never moved.

Frosty was built for the other half: the doing.

HOW IT WORKS

Plan, execute, deliver, approve.

The recurring work that eats a team's week: recapping calls, triaging inboxes, drafting follow-ups, standing reports. One-off, or on a schedule that repeats without anyone remembering to trigger it.

  • Ask: a task goes in from Slack or the app
  • Plan: Frosty writes a visible plan first, so nothing happens in a black box
  • Execute: the work runs through the tools the team already uses, rather than asking them to move
  • Deliver: the finished result comes back for human approval
THE GUARDRAILS

Autonomy with a leash.

Anything consequential stops for a human decision before it goes out. Pricing is shared workspace credits with a hard cap rather than per-seat licensing, so cost tracks work performed, not headcount.

Built for agencies, startups and small teams across operations, marketing, sales and support.

WHAT IT PROVES

The AI coworker is a product, not a slide.

The AI page on this site describes God Mode: an AI coworker on your whole business that does things rather than only answering questions. HeyFrosty is that idea, shipped. When we install an AI-Powered Growth System in a client's business, it is built by people who have already shipped one of their own and run it every day.
