Smart Sellers Academy

From scratch to $225K/mo · the cleanest ramp we've run.

Engaged January 2025 with no GTM infrastructure of any kind. By the end of the year the system was running on its own — and the run rate hit $225K/mo, the highest of any partnership we'd installed at that stage.

THE SITUATION

Engaged with nothing in place.

January 2025. Smart Sellers Academy had a product, an audience, and zero infrastructure to convert one into the other. No defined lead system. No structured sales process. No visibility into what was working — because nothing was being tracked. Revenue was coming in by accident.

Every founder we work with has a different starting point. Smart Sellers started with the cleanest blank slate of any of them — which, counter-intuitively, made the install faster.

WHAT WE BUILT

The complete GTM, refined.

By this point we'd already built and refined this same install across two previous partnerships. Smart Sellers got the cleaned-up version.

  • Lead generation system — funnel architecture, capture flow, qualification
  • Sales process — discovery call → application → close, fully scripted
  • CRM pipeline — every stage, every automation, every SMS + email cadence
  • KPI reporting — weekly dashboards, conversion-rate tracking at every step
  • Team handoff — the system was built FOR Smart Sellers, then handed to them to run
THE OUTCOME

$1.3M in 15 months. $225K/mo avg.

The faster ramp than Acquisition Network wasn't a coincidence — the system was more refined by then, and Smart Sellers was a cleaner slate to install onto.

Total generated: $1.3M in just over 15 months, with the run rate at $225K/mo at the time of handoff. The team owns and operates the system today.
