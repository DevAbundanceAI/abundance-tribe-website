THE THESIS

Own the whole engine.

Most health brands rent their growth: an agency for ads, a freelancer for the site, a tool for email, and nobody accountable for the whole. Expert Health was built the opposite way. One system, designed together, operated by the people who own the outcome.

It is also the proof behind everything else on this site. Nothing gets sold to a client that has not already been made to work here.

WHAT WE BUILT

Storefront to content engine.

Everything below was designed and shipped in-house, on one locked design system.

  • Brand system and a product imagery pipeline built to look real rather than generated
  • A conversion-designed storefront and product pages, mobile first, twelve products across nine goal categories
  • A scored health assessment funnel that branches into personalised result paths and routes qualified leads into the CRM
  • Forty-eight ready-to-run ad units across Meta, search, short-form video and professional channels, each compliance-flagged before it runs
  • Per-product science pages built for Google and for the AI answer engines buyers now ask first
  • An automated social content engine posting across platforms from a single schedule
THE CONSTRAINT

Compliance shapes everything.

Health is a regulated category. What a product page may claim, which ad copy clears review, how a testimonial can be used: all of it is constrained, and all of it got built into the workflow rather than bolted on after. Claims are reviewed before they publish. Ad copy is flagged before it runs.

If you are launching in a regulated space, this is the part that stops most teams. We have already been through it, on our own money.

WHAT IT PROVES

The AI search channel is real.

The per-product science pages were built for the AI answer engines as much as for Google, and that bet paid. Buyers ask ChatGPT and Perplexity which telehealth brand to use, and the answer is increasingly this one. It is why AI search visibility sits inside every Scale engagement we run now: not because we read about it, but because it is on our own dashboard.
