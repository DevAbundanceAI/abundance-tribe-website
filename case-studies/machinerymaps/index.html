MachineryMaps

One codebase. Vendor one of many.

Heavy equipment rental is booked by phone across thousands of local operators, and nobody shows real inventory across them. MachineryMaps is the marketplace that will. It is being built as the same product as the ON-SITE storefront, so the first vendor proves the model with a real fleet and the platform opens to the next operators without a rebuild.

THE SITUATION

A category still booked by phone.

A contractor who needs a crane tomorrow calls three yards, waits on three callbacks, and takes whichever one answers first. Operators hold real inventory and real availability and none of it is visible anywhere a buyer can act on it.

The opportunity is not another brochure site per operator. It is one place where real inventory across operators is bookable the way anything else is bookable.

WHAT WE BUILT

Built once, architected for many.

Everything that ships for ON-SITE ships on the marketplace codebase. Nothing gets developed twice.

  • Equipment listings with real availability
  • Quote capture that routes into the CRM instead of a voicemail
  • Insurance upload and verification inside the order flow
  • E-signature on rental agreements, deposits held on card
  • Owner approval before any charge fires
  • An order state machine designed for multiple vendors from day one
WHERE IT GOES

From one fleet to the category.

ON-SITE proves the model first, on its own machines and its own customers. Once vendor one is transacting, the platform opens to other Phoenix operators, then beyond. Contractors browse real inventory across yards and book it. Operators get orders instead of voicemails.

This is the engagement that shows the product side of the work: not funnels, but software that takes money, and the marketing that sends people to it designed at the same time.
