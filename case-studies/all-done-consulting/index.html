All Done Consulting

$4.6M built across 2.5 years.

All Done Consulting had no existing GTM infrastructure when we started. Two and a half years and four major system builds later, the engine had produced $4.6M and was fully owned by the team.

THE SITUATION

A consulting business with no marketing system.

All Done Consulting was generating revenue, but every dollar required the founder's direct involvement. There was no funnel, no nurture system, no launch infrastructure, no email program, no CRM — just a calendar and a network. The longest-lived partnership we'd run started as the messiest.

WHAT WE BUILT

Four full systems over 2.5 years.

This wasn't a single 90-day install — it was a multi-year embedded relationship. Across the partnership we built and rebuilt the GTM stack as the business grew.

  • Webinar funnels — repeatable launch infrastructure that ran without the founder
  • Book-a-call funnels — for high-ticket consulting offers
  • Email automation — segmented nurture, abandoned-cart, post-purchase, win-back
  • Full launch systems — multi-week campaigns with assets, scripts, ads, and analytics
  • Operating cadence — weekly KPI reviews, monthly system audits, quarterly strategy
THE OUTCOME

$4.6M and a team that runs the engine.

By the end of the 2.5-year partnership, All Done Consulting had generated $4.6M through the systems we built — but the more durable outcome was the team. They run every funnel, every launch, every campaign without us in the loop. The system is theirs.

This is what an Abundance install looks like when it runs long: not dependency, but transfer. The system, the playbooks, and the operating cadence are all owned by the client at handoff.
