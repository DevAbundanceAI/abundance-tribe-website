FAQ

Everything you'll probably email us about.

How long is the contract?

Install is one-time. Continuity (Shift or Scale) is month-to-month after that — cancel anytime, 30-day notice.

Do I need an existing offer?

Yes — we install marketing infrastructure, we don't invent your offer. If you don't have an offer yet, the Brand OS audit is the better starting point.

What ad spend should I plan for?

$3K/mo minimum to see the system work as designed. Most clients run $5–15K/mo by month 3.

Can I keep my current copywriter?

Yes, but most clients let theirs go after seeing what our team ships. We can collaborate either way.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes — UK, Canada, Australia, EU. Anywhere Stripe + Meta both operate.

What if the ads don't perform?

Tuesday optimization calls. New creative every 14 days. We don't stop until CPL is in range.
