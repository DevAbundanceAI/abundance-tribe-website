What is in it

Nine systems. One engine.

These are not a menu. They are the stages of one engine, and the constraint is always sitting in one of them. We install the one that is capping you first, then the next.

1

Offer and positioning

What you sell, to whom, at what promise, and why now. The one stage nothing downstream can compensate for, so it is where every install starts.

2

Meta ads

Creative built from what has actually converted, audiences built from your own data, and a feedback loop from the CRM back into the account so spend follows closes, not clicks.

3

Google ads

Search and local PPC run on a documented method: keyword research, structure and bidding, conversion tracking, quality score, and a red-team checklist before anything goes live.

4

Creative and organic

Scripts and short-form built from a library of what is winning in your market, in your voice, on a production board your team can actually run.

5

SEO and AI search

Showing up in Google, and inside ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity when your buyers ask them who to use. Page structure, answer blocks, and the technical crawlability underneath.

6

Funnels, VSLs and applications

The page, the video, the application and the follow-up sequence that turn a stranger into a booked call, with the qualification built in so your calendar fills with the right people.

7

CRM, pipeline and follow-up

Every stage, every automation, every reminder. Leads followed up within minutes even when a rep drops the ball, and reactivation campaigns on the pipeline that went quiet.

8

Sales process and call intelligence

Scripts, SOPs, and the real numbers: true show rate, true close rate, and whether the lead marked unqualified actually was.

9

Dashboards and attribution

One warehouse that pulls the ad platforms, the CRM and the forms together, so the weekly decision is made on numbers everyone trusts.
