Common questions.

Everything founders ask before booking a Strategy Call. If yours isn't here, the call is the place to ask it.

How is this priced?

Two upfront tiers depending on scope — Launch (ads + funnel DFY) or Install (full system including CRM). Both are one-time investments paid upfront, with Klarna and Affirm financing available. After your 3-month build, optional month-to-month continuity (Shift coaching or Scale agency). Full pricing structure is walked through on the Strategy Call — that way we can fit the right tier to your situation.

How fast will I see results?

Ads launch within 14 days of kickoff. Most founders see first booked calls within the first week of ad spend. Meaningful pipeline lift typically shows up between day 27 and day 60. Full compounding by day 90.

What do I have to do? What do you do?

You onboard with us (one kickoff call, a few async questions) and film your own ad creative (we provide the scripts and direction). We do everything else — offer architecture, sales funnel, copywriting, video editing, tracking, CRM setup, ad account configuration, and media buying for the first 90 days.

Do I need an existing offer?

You need a service you can deliver to clients — coaching, consulting, agency work, done-for-you services. We'll refine the offer positioning, pricing, and packaging as part of the install. You don't need a fully baked funnel — that's what we build.

How much should I expect to spend on ads?

Plan for a meaningful ad budget during the install and first 90 days — we'll talk through the right monthly spend for your goals on the Strategy Call. Ad spend is separate from the install investment and goes directly to Meta. We optimize it in real time as the data comes in.

What if it doesn't work for me?

The 30-min Strategy Call is the qualifier — we don't take clients we don't think we can win for. If we don't believe the system will work for your offer, we won't sell you the install. Once you're in, we're committed to making it work — that's why ongoing continuity is optional, not required.

What happens after the 3-month build?

Two optional continuity paths. Shift is continuity coaching — ongoing group coaching, templates, and the founder community. Scale is ongoing agency — continued consulting, fresh scripts and copy, and active media buying. Or you can run the system on your own — we hand off everything cleanly. Both continuity options are month-to-month and discussed in detail on the Strategy Call.

Why a 30-minute call instead of just buying online?

Two reasons. First, this is a real investment — you should talk to the operator before committing. Second, we need to make sure your offer and business model are a fit before we say yes. The call is half qualification, half clarity audit. You'll leave with insights either way.
