COMMON QUESTIONS

What founders ask before signing.

How is Abundance different from a typical marketing agency?
Most agencies do one thing — ads, or funnels, or coaching. Abundance installs the entire Selling System end-to-end: ad creative, funnels, CRM, tracking, sales scripts, and ongoing coaching. We replace six hires with one team that runs the whole stack.
What do you need from me to get started?
Two things: show up on camera for the ad creative we'll script for you, and complete a 90-minute onboarding so we can document your offer, ICP, and sales process. Everything else — copy, design, funnel, CRM, tracking — we handle.
What if my offer isn't ready yet?
Apply anyway. We'll tell you on the strategy call whether your offer is ready, or what to sharpen first. If your offer isn't there yet, we won't take your money — we'll tell you exactly what to fix and invite you back in 60 days.
Is there a minimum ad budget?
We recommend a minimum of $3,000/mo in ad spend for the system to compound. Below that, the data is too noisy to learn from. We'll talk through the math on the call so you know what to expect.
What's the difference between Launch and Install?
Launch ($9k) gets your ads + funnel live in 14 days; you keep your current CRM and we consult on how to wire it up. Install ($12k) includes everything in Launch plus a full GoHighLevel CRM build, automations, pipeline, and reporting — done for you, not by you.
Do I have to commit to monthly continuity?
No. The upfront tiers (Launch / Install) are one-time. Continuity tiers (Shift / Scale) start at month 4 and are month-to-month — you can leave at any time. Most clients stay because the system compounds the longer it runs.
Who actually does the work?
Frosty (founder) leads strategy + sales. A small senior team handles creative, copy, and ops. We don't outsource to an offshore studio — every deliverable is built by someone who's installed the system before.
