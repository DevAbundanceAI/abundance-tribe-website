The Final 6 · Locked palette

Six colors. Six roles.

Every page picks colors from this six. Each has a single role — pages rotate which colors appear in which sections, but no new colors get introduced. All six are 4-stop gradients on a 135° angle, cream → deep.

01 Peach Primary

The signature. Logo mark, hero gradient titles, primary CTAs, footer mark, page bookends. Use on every page.

#F5F0E8 #F8C5A8 #E8967E #C4715E
02 Coral Warm Sibling

Community, urgency, energy moments. Tribe sections, secondary CTAs, callouts that need more punch than peach.

#F5F0E8 #FECACA #FB7185 #BE123C
03 Copper Bronze Depth

Heritage moments, case studies, long-form essays, Partner tier, Freedom offer. The "earned" warm.

#F5F0E8 #F2D4B8 #B87333 #6B4120

05 Cerulean Cool Accent

Features, product UI, data visualization, integrations, technical sections. The single cool hue per page — use sparingly.

#F5F0E8 #B8DDEC #2A7FB8 #1A4A6B

Pricing, FAQ, legal, comparison tables, dense data. Where the page needs to feel quiet and serious.

The Glow Theory

4-stop cream → deep. Always 135°.

Every glow gradient follows the same template: cream at 0%, a pale tint at 40%, the saturated mid at 75%, the deep anchor at 100%. The angle is always 135° (top-left to bottom-right). That consistency across all six colors is what makes the system feel like one system.

Rules of Engagement

Don't break these.

The system holds together because every page follows the same rules. Each one prevents a specific failure mode we've seen.

01

Six colors only

Peach, Coral, Copper, Mocha, Cerulean, Slate. No new colors get introduced. No exceptions.

02

Dark mode only

Every brand surface is matte black. The luminous-glow mechanic doesn't translate to light bg. Light interfaces are a separate system.

03

One em per page

Neo Contact + peach gradient + own line + 1.2× parent size. Once per page max. Use it for the moment that matters most.

04

One glow per section

Each section picks one of the six glows for its radial bg + em color. No section mixes two glows. Hero is always Peach.

05

135° gradients, always

All glow gradients use a 135° angle, top-left to bottom-right. Don't rotate. Don't go radial except for ambient hero halos.

06

4-stop pattern

Cream 0% → pale tint 40% → saturated mid 75% → deep anchor 100%. Don't add stops. Don't drop the cream start.

07

20 / 10 / 100 radii

20px for cards/pills/highlights. 10px for buttons. 100px for pill labels (badges, urgency bars). No other radii.

08

Greycliff is the default

All UI, body, and headlines use Greycliff CF. Neo Contact only inside <em> within h1/h2/h3. Never elsewhere.

09

Color from content

The brand carries the gradient. Color variety comes from imagery — headshots, product screenshots, abstract art — not from adding more brand colors.

10

Buttons always carry peach

Primary fills the gradient. Ghost borders it. Cream replaces it on Coral/Cerulean sections only. No button without a gradient relationship.

Logos

Wordmark lockups

Light wordmark on matte black is the default — that's the only mode. If a light surface is ever needed (email signature, print), use the dark wordmark on cream.
