FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?+
Established founders and operators doing $30K–$500K/mo in revenue who already have a working offer and want to scale acquisition without hiring a marketing team in-house. Best fit: coaches, consultants, agencies, and high-ticket service businesses.
How long does it take to get live?+
Ads are running within 14 days of kickoff. Full CRM, funnel, and retargeting ecosystem is live within 30 days. You stay focused on closing — we handle the build.
How quickly can I see results?+
First booked appointments typically land in week two or three. Meaningful close-rate movement and CPL optimization is usually a 60–90 day curve as we learn your buyer and tune the system.
What makes this different from a traditional ads agency?+
Traditional agencies run ads. We build the full Selling System: brand positioning, creative, funnel, CRM, follow-up automation, and the data layer that ties it together. The ads are one layer of five — not the whole engagement.
Is there a minimum contract?+
Yes. Acquisition systems compound — the first 60 days are infrastructure, the next 60 are optimization. We work in 90-day commitments so the data has room to mature. After that, month-to-month.
Is this done-for-you or done-with-you?+
Done-for-you on the build and the operation. The only things we need from you are access, your offer materials, and time on camera for ad creative. We handle the rest end-to-end.
