Test page · what this is

This is a sandbox to react to a single-signature blood-orange direction before committing. The locked Final 6 system in BRAND.md is untouched. Nothing about this page overrides the existing tokens, the per-section glow rotation, or the dark-mode-only rule on the rest of the site.

Three open questions worth answering before any rollout: (1) Does tiger's blood feel premium or sales-bro at hero scale? (2) Does the cool data-viz blue accent feel necessary, or can the gradient carry everything? (3) Is light mode actually load-bearing for the business, or is it a nice-to-have we're chasing?
