FAQ

Common questions.

The ones that come up before every kickoff call.

How long until I see results? +
First booked appointments typically land in week 2. Meaningful close-rate movement and CPL optimization is a 60–90 day curve as the system learns your buyer.
Is there a minimum contract? +
Yes — 90 days. The first 60 are infrastructure, the next 30 are optimization. After that, month-to-month with 30-day notice.
What's done-for-you vs done-with-you? +
Done-for-you on the build and the operation. We need access, your offer materials, and ~2 hours/wk of your time on camera for ad creative. Everything else is on us.
What if my ad spend is small? +
Minimum effective spend is $3K/mo on Meta — below that, the algorithm doesn't get enough signal. If you're under that, the Tribe tier is the better fit until your revenue supports the spend.
Can I see a sample dashboard? +
Yes — on the strategy call. We'll walk through a redacted client account showing the exact tracking, attribution, and reporting setup you'd have.
What if it's not working after 90 days? +
The performance guarantee: if you haven't recouped your investment in 90 days, we work for free until you do. We win when you win.
