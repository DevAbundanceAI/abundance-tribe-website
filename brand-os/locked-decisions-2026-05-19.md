# Locked Decisions — 2026-05-19

VSL + ICP + offer architecture decisions locked during the post-Codespace-crash strategic conversation.

---

## ICP — Language and segmentation

- **Use specific types, NOT "service founder."** "Service founder" is industry jargon — cold prospects pause to decode it. Use **"coaches, consultants, agency owners"** — Cole-style. Prospects self-identify in milliseconds.

- **Revenue band: stay at $15K–$60K/mo.** Matches case study evidence (every Abundance partnership scaled FROM this range UP via the install). Above $60K, founders have teams — the "you're the bottleneck" psychology weakens. Higher-revenue capture goes to Unlocked ($18K) and Freedom ($60K), already in the offer ladder.

- **Three ICP psychology segments** all addressed:
  1. **Ceiling-of-organic** founders (built up via referrals, hit the cap)
  2. **Burned-by-agency** founders (tried agency, didn't work — high intent, already convinced ads work)
  3. **Currently-running-unprofitable-ads** founders (actively spending, can't crack profitable ROAS)

  These three segments share a common state: *they want paid traffic to work but it hasn't yet.* All three are addressed in the VSL Beat 7 tactics graveyard + Beat 8 mechanism intro.

- **Behavioral filter: "sold at least 20 clients ONLY"** — on the LP headline only (Cole VSL #2 pattern). NOT in VSL audio. Pre-validates the offer + filters tire-kickers.

## VSL — Structural locks

- **Template:** Cole VSL #2 (7-9 min, 12 beats, DFY install positioning) — structurally closest to Abundance's offer.

- **Hook formula:** Cole 3-in-1 — action + result + guarantee + ICP filter in ~30 seconds.

- **P3 receipts-first:** Lead with the WORK ($12M+ across 5 partnerships, named case studies), introduce founder name SECOND. Pure authority, never vulnerability/personal-revenue framing.

- **Length target:** 7-9 minutes (~1,150-1,300 words).

- **Guarantee specificity per surface:**
  - **VSL audio:** vague-but-confident ("predictable qualified appointments month after month. Or you don't pay.")
  - **LP headline:** Sabri-range ("10–30+ qualified appointments per month, scaled to ad spend") with asterisk → details on call
  - **Strategy Call:** full dynamic spend-tier table revealed
  - **Contract:** signed specifics + 5 qualification criteria

- **Specific appointment numbers stay OUT of VSL audio.** Matches Cole VSL #2's pattern for structurally similar offers (his ads/install LP also stays vague: "if we don't perform, you don't pay").

- **DO NOT APPLY disqualifier:** under-CTA on LP only, NOT in VSL audio (Sabri pattern — keeps VSL flow tight).

---

## Guarantee — Dynamic spend-tier math

- **Locked range:** "10 to 30 qualified appointments per month" based on Meta ad spend
- **Math:** $5K/mo spend → 10 qualified/mo, $10K → 20/mo, $15K → 30/mo. Implied CPBC ~$500 holding constant across tiers (defensible for high-ticket B2B with strict 5-criteria qualification).
- **Above $15K spend:** custom math, walked through on Strategy Call.
- **Visible where:** LP (with asterisk), Strategy Call (full table), contract (signed specifics). NOT in VSL audio.
- **5 signed qualification criteria** (defines "qualified appointment"):
  1. Matches ICP
  2. Decision-maker confirmed
  3. Expressed offer interest via funnel
  4. Budget-qualified
  5. Confirmed booking via Triple Threat Appointment Confirmation
- **Explicit carve-outs:** NOT guaranteeing show rate, close rate, or revenue per close.

## Financing — The follow-up line

- **Locked phrasing for VSL:** *"Plus, if you're qualified, you could get this entire system installed for as low as zero dollars down."*
- **3 placements in the VSL:** after the cold-open hook, at the mid-VSL CTA, at the final guarantee restate (Beats 1, 5, 10).
- **Don't explain HOW financing works in the VSL.** Save Klarna/Affirm mechanics for the pre-consult video and Strategy Call.
- **Compliance hedging (locked Brand Voice Rule 3):** "as low as $0 down for qualifying applicants" — required hedge. NEVER flat "$0 down" or "pay after results" framing.

## Step 1 expansion — Inevitable Offer System

The Step 1 deliverable now explicitly includes BOTH:

1. **Reposition the client's ticket** to where paid-ads math works (typically $7,500+).
2. **Install financing infrastructure on the CLIENT's offer** (Klarna/Affirm/equivalent) so qualifying prospects can start with as low as $0 down on their offer.

This is huge because:
- Widens the client's qualified-prospect pool (more people technically can afford)
- Eat-our-own-cooking twice (we use financing on our offer + we install it on yours)
- Most agencies won't touch financing — becomes a differentiator
- Strengthens the math behind Abundance's guarantee at the client level

## Funnel — Multi-gate qualification architecture

Four progressively tighter gates as prospect moves from cold → close:

1. **Cold ad** → broad ICP types (coach/consultant/agency)
2. **VSL audio** → "high-ticket" + ICP psychology (broad TAM)
3. **LP headline** → "Sold At Least 20 Clients ONLY" (Sabri filter)
4. **Vibe-coded application** → revenue + ticket + client count + decision-maker + budget readiness (hard gates with grading built in)
5. **Pre-consult video** → reinforces logic, walks dimensional pain (Sabri 6:47am-style)
6. **Strategy Call** → math walkthrough + final fit determination
