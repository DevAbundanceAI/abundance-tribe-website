## Changes from v21

**Architectural — agenda + body restructure to 3-act DR:**

1. **Agenda goes from 4 hooks + bonus → 3 hooks + bonus.** The "ONE thing missing" hook (v21 hook 3) is dropped as a separate hook. Its concept was already present in the bonus tease ("the one piece every $10M+ business runs on that almost nobody at your level builds for themselves") — keeping both was redundant.

   New 3-hook structure:
   1. **Why everything you've tried hasn't worked** (problem) — Pain stack + anti-agency + AI slop + AI tools
   2. **The paradox** (insight) — easier AND harder simultaneously, structural meta-pattern
   3. **The solution we built** (solution) — Mechanism
   + **Bonus** — DATA piece (unchanged from v21)

2. **Body reordered to match: Pain → Paradox+EVERYTHING → Mechanism.**
   - **Beat 6 (Pain)** now contains: VA/course/agency + Meta ROAS + sales team/Mr. Big Baby Closer/Marketing Gods + AI slop + AI agencies + AI FOMO + AI tools. Full tactical-failure stack.
   - **Beat 7 (Paradox + EVERYTHING)** now contains: pure structural paradox (easier/harder/two fires) + EVERYTHING reveal as the diagnostic punchline. No AI slop content here — moved to Pain where it belongs as a tactical failure, not a structural insight.
   - **Beat 8 (Mechanism)** unchanged.

3. **EVERYTHING reveal works as a surprise punchline** instead of a pre-loaded payoff. Without hook 3 in the agenda, the EVERYTHING moment lands as an AHA inside the Paradox beat rather than a telegraphed reveal. Stronger DR psychology.

4. **AI slop + AI FOMO relocated** from v21's Paradox beat into v22's Pain beat. AI slop is a tactical failure ("you tried AI agencies, they generated slop") — belongs in Pain. The Paradox beat is now purely structural.

**All Frosty content preserved verbatim:**
- All v18-v21 inline additions
- Operating System Step 5 rename + "Every Cost" + "Optimize. And Scale." rhythm
- Sales leadership in Step 4
- Mindset closer
- Hook 3 reframe verbiage ("wasting time and money trying to solve this on their own") — now folded into hook 1's framing
