That distinction matters: Beat 1 sold them on the outcome; Beat 4 sells them on staying for the explanation. Each agenda item is a curiosity loop that gets closed in the body:

- Item 1 (easier to scale) → opens. Closed implicitly in Beat 8 (mechanism — the modern leverage Abundance OS provides).
- Item 2 (harder to land clients) → opens. Closed in Beat 7 (pain — the consistent-clients problem dimensionalized).
- Item 3 (what doesn't work / pattern) → opens. Closed in Beat 8 (anti-agency block — the pattern is "pieces from disconnected teams" + AI slop).
- Item 4 (what we do differently) → opens. Closed in Beat 8 (mechanism walkthrough + 5 Steps).

If a v13 wants to tighten further, Item 1 ("easier than ever to scale") is the weakest payoff — it doesn't have a strong specific landing point in the body.
