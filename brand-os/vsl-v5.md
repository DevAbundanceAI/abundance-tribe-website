## Why the change matters

The phrases removed were meta-narrative — they CALLED OUT that Abundance OS is "the unique mechanism." Confident copy never labels its rhetorical moves. Cole/Sabri/Hormozi all describe their mechanisms with confidence and let the prospect arrive at "this is different" on their own.

v5 keeps Abundance OS just as prominent — it's still named in Beats 2, 6, 8 (mechanism walkthrough across 5 Steps), 9, and the wrap line. The prospect still concludes it's the differentiator. We just don't announce the conclusion ourselves.
