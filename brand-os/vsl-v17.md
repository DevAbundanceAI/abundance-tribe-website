## What changed structurally

This is not an inline edit pass. This is a structural rewrite based on the senior-DR diagnosis: each beat now does ONE thing. Cuts ~3.5 min of runtime. Same offer. Same proof. Same guarantee.

**Major changes from v16:**

1. **One brand name throughout: Abundance AI.** Abundance OS does not appear in the audio. The company name doubles as the product name — every "Abundance AI" mention compounds brand equity. OS reintroduces later (SaaS marketing, in-product copy, Deep Dive).
2. **Wrap line evolved to W2:** *"One Foundation. Five Steps. Built by Abundance AI. Finished by humans."*
3. **5 Steps renamed to concrete verbs** (locked architectural scope preserved):
   - Build the Offer → **Dial in your offer**
   - Attract the Buyer → **Get your ads in front of buyers**
   - Pre-Sell the Appointment → **Pre-sell every prospect before they hit your calendar**
   - Close the Client → **Make every sales call easier to close**
   - Optimize the Engine → **See the data. Find the leak.**
4. **"You film, we edit" line lives inside Step 2** — single best expectation-setting move in the entire VSL.
5. **12 Systems → teased only.** Single line: "Plus — under those 5 Steps live 10 distinct systems… you'll see them inside the Deep Dive after you apply."
6. **"We use it on ourselves first" moved to Beat 2** — strongest single credibility move pulled forward to anchor trust before any proof block.
7. **Founder credibility upgrade in Beat 5** — the $12M aggregate stat moves from Beat 3 (where it was anonymous proof) to Beat 5 (where it anchors founder authority).
8. **ICP framing on each Beat 3 case study** — placeholders need your fill-in (see notes).
9. **Honest timeline acknowledgment in Beat 8** — single sentence: "Install completes in 14 days. First appointments typically arrive in weeks 3-6 as the ads optimize. By day 90, you have predictable monthly volume." Kills the most likely refund complaint.
10. **Soft ICP qualifier in Beat 1 audio** — "already past $15K a month" — tightens application quality without the harsh LP disqualifier.
11. **Pain section (Beat 6)** preserves Mr. Big Baby Closer + phone-grab + Stripe-refresh + EVERYTHING-problem reframe. Trimmed redundancy (the "running four or five disconnected things" paragraph collapsed).
12. **Mechanism (Beat 7) replaces v16's 290-sec Beat 8** — single clean explanation of Brand DNA capture + AI training + 5 concrete steps. ~160 sec vs 290.

**Concepts I cut without consensus — flagged for your review:**

- **Brain-washing line (was in v16 Beat 6).** Tactical risk: at front of VSL where trust is fragile, "you're being brain-washed" is a coin flip. Cut for v17. Restore if you disagree — the line itself is fine, just risky in this position.
- **ROAS meta-aside in Beat 5 (v16: "I'm talking ROAS the number one thing you should be focused on…").** Cut as rhythm-breaker. The "$1 in / $2-$12 out" line already does the work.
- **Over-pitching / Yoda block (v16 Beat 8).** Cut entirely. Relocate to Reels content ideas. Teaches copywriting craft, doesn't help a coach decide to apply.
- **"AI-Powered Ads Selling System" → "AI-Powered Selling System."** Dropped the "Ads" qualifier — three modifiers in one noun phrase was too much, and "Ads" made the offer sound narrower than it actually is.
- **"Pre-sold prospects" rant in v16 Beat 9.** Cut. Implied in "pre-sold qualified appointments" line in re-offer.
- **The 12 systems list in v16 Beat 10.** Cut entirely from audio. Teased once.
