Before you book

The questions everyone asks first.

Are you an agency?
Not in the way that word usually means. An agency owns a channel and bills you for it every month, and it stops where its invoice stops. We are accountable for the whole engine, we install the fix with your team, and we leave them owning it. If the honest answer is that you do not need anything from us yet, you get told that on the call.
What does "the entire go-to-market engine" actually include?
Everything between a stranger and a paying customer, and the software underneath it. Offer and positioning. Ads, creative, organic and search. The funnel, the video, the application, the follow-up. CRM, pipeline and sales process. Websites, storefronts and custom tools. And the AI layer that makes all of it cheaper to run. The three lane pages break each part down.
Can we hire you for just one piece?
Yes, and most engagements start that way. We still begin from your numbers rather than from the piece you asked for, because the thing you noticed is often not the thing that is capping you. If it turns out the ads are fine and the sales process is the leak, we say so before you spend on ads.
Do you build software or just marketing?
Both, on purpose. Marketing that lands on a site that cannot take an order is wasted. Software nobody sends traffic to is a hobby. The ON-SITE Rentals and MachineryMaps case studies are the build side, the five growth installs are the marketing side, and Expert Health is both at once.
What actually happens on the discovery call?
Thirty minutes on your business. You leave with three things. Which stage of the engine is most likely capping you. Whether your business is ready for what you are asking for, and if it is not, what has to go in first. And a straight answer on whether working together is worth it. You keep all three either way.
What does it cost?
We talk about that on the call, once we both know what we are actually fixing. Putting a number on this page before that would be guessing, and you would have no way to tell whether it was worth it.
